## Topic Memory: Team Dynamics & Collaboration
_Last updated: 2026-11-03_

## Established findings
- Diversity pays off only through process, and specifically through information crossing the fault line: identity-only training moved nothing while identity-plus-elaboration training raised creativity (W7218884614, 2026-10); cross-cultural elaboration beat same-culture elaboration for performance (W7221436866, 2026-10).
- Peer status dynamics, not formal authority, drive much team behavior: hierarchical teams block high-status additions after failure (W7203807114, 2026-08); quiet newcomers get hazed unless they hold group status (W7215049715, 2026-10); peer injustice spills into blame of the supervisor (W7205007222, 2026-09).
- Standout performers draw peer punishment, which they then reciprocate: public gratitude to one employee triggers deprivation, expediency and self-promotion in observers (W7202283587, 2026-08); individual job crafting triggers coworker undermining (W7205707681, 2026-09); incivility leads high-proactivity employees to feel envied and turn political (W7221801860, 2026-10).
- Informal talk is a work resource, not noise: truthful, work-related gossip speeds newcomer adjustment (W7207711849, 2026-09); workplace parenting conversations benefit work and home (W7212308571, 2026-09).
- Team states differ in malleability: affect shifts easily, conflict hardens, and performance feeds back into later states and processes (W7220392932, 2026-10 meta-analysis).

## Emerging threads
- Timing/back-loaded returns: robot teammates only outperformed in later project phases (W7223169561, 2026-10); kickoff scaffolding paid off in grants and papers much later (W7222970844, 2026-10); co-mover team penalty partly faded over time (W7221144841, 2026-10). Watch for replication outside sports and biomedical settings.
- Machines as teammates: acceptance hinges on human-likeness, expectation-competence match, and reactive rather than proactive help (W7223169561, 2026-10) — first appearance in this topic.
- Biometric/"objective" team measurement is not ready: physiological synchrony metrics disagree, sometimes in opposite directions (W7213563235, 2026-09).
- Group entry as a social unit (co-movers, lift-outs) tensions with earlier finding that a well-chosen addition helps struggling flat teams (W7221144841 vs W7203807114) — unresolved.
- Configural and contingent team measures gaining ground: cognitive isolation only harms under low epistemic motivation (W7211946634, 2026-09); planned vs improvised adaptation depends on change novelty (W7214778895, 2026-09).
- Persistent methodological limit: self-report, single-source, student and lab samples; field experiments/quasi-experiments (W7218884614, W7212308571, W7222970844) remain the exception.
