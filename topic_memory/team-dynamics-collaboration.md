# Topic Memory: Team Dynamics & Collaboration
_Last updated: 2026-10-04_

## Established findings
- Peer-level status dynamics, not formal authority, drive much of team behavior: hierarchical teams block high-status additions after failure (W7203807114, 2026-08), quiet newcomers are hazed unless they hold group status (W7215049715, 2026-10), peer injustice from high-status colleagues spills over into blame of the supervisor (W7205007222, 2026-09).
- Informal talk functions as a work resource rather than noise: truthful, work-related gossip speeds newcomer norm clarity and integration (W7207711849, 2026-09); workplace parenting conversations benefit both work and home (W7212308571, 2026-09).
- Manager interventions generate bystander side effects: public gratitude toward one employee triggers relative deprivation, expediency and self-promotion in observers (W7202283587, 2026-08); individual job crafting triggers both inspiration and anxiety-driven undermining in coworkers (W7205707681, 2026-09).
- Diversity benefits require process, not attitudes: identity-only training moved nothing; identity plus information-elaboration training raised creativity in highly gender-diverse teams (W7218884614, 2026-10).

## Emerging threads
- Team training may distribute learning unequally by role; the coordinator/"Organizer" learns least (W7202035170, 2026-08) — watch for field replication beyond student samples.
- Configural rather than average cognitive measures: "cognitive isolation" of a single member harms performance only under low epistemic motivation (W7211946634, 2026-09).
- Contingent adaptation: planned adaptation suits minor/familiar change, improvisation suits novel/disruptive change, with goal orientation predicting which teams use (W7214778895, 2026-09).
- Male allyship evidence suggests feared backlash is modest; managers' motives read as job-related vs. non-managers' empathic concern (W7214919069, 2026-09) — small, self-selected qualitative base needing confirmation.
- Recurring methodological limit across this topic: self-report, single-source and student-team designs; field experiments (W7218884614, W7212308571) are the exception.

## Feature ideas already pitched
