## Emerging threads
- Adversity imprints durably on ventures rather than simply strengthening or killing them: disaster-exposed startups keep flatter, generalist structures for a decade with higher failure risk plus occasional IPO upside (W7220410857, 2026-10-05).
- Resilience is being reframed by qualitative researchers as cyclical endurance with hidden costs ("rhythmic resilience," "resilience–survival paradox") rather than growth through hardship (W7203797024, 2026-08-19).
- Founder identity under stigma translates directly into venture strategy — mission, hiring, branding — producing divergent business models from the same social disadvantage (W7214760936, 2026-09-29).
- Entrepreneurial self-disclosure online is managed labor with gendered risk, not spontaneous authenticity; multiple simultaneous motives (self, relational, normative) (W7215047180, 2026-10-02).
- Ecosystem policy trade-off: resource-attracting policies raise regional output but drain peripheries and increase fragility; resource-embedding policies trade output for spatial inclusion (W7204156044, 2026-08-25).
- Methodological note: this batch leaned heavily qualitative/longitudinal field work and historical case analysis; watch whether quantitative replication follows.
