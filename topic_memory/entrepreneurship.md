# Topic Memory: Entrepreneurship
_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

## Established findings
- Founders' and owners' readings of how others see them — or what others believe — translate directly into firm behavior: stigma-driven identity choices shape mission, hiring and branding (W7214760936, 2026-09-29); misread peer beliefs predict fewer sustainability policies (W7223102244, 2026-10-20); anticipated audience judgment shapes women founders' disclosure tactics (W7215047180, 2026-10-02).
- Adversity imprints durably on ventures rather than simply strengthening or killing them: disaster-exposed startups keep flatter, generalist structures for a decade, with higher failure risk plus occasional IPO upside (W7220410857, 2026-10-05).

## Emerging threads
- Resilience reframed as cyclical endurance with hidden costs ("rhythmic resilience," "resilience–survival paradox") rather than growth through hardship (W7203797024, 2026-08-19).
- Ecosystem policy trade-off: resource-attracting policies raise regional output but drain peripheries and raise fragility; resource-embedding policies trade output for spatial inclusion (W7204156044, 2026-08-25).
- Pluralistic ignorance / collective misperception as a low-cost policy lever in the SME sector — correlational so far; watch for an experimental belief-correction test (W7223102244).
- Equifinality in motivation: passion as multiple distinct configurations, differing between entrepreneurs and employees and between harmonious and obsessive forms (W7225316879, 2026-11-05) — implies generic engagement programs underperform.
- Climate and sustainability entering entrepreneurship research as a behavioral/psychological problem rather than a policy or technology one.
- Method shift underway: the Aug–Oct 2026 batch was qualitative/longitudinal/historical; the Oct–Nov batch brings a multi-country registered report and configurational fsQCA. Watch whether large-N and preregistered designs keep displacing field ethnography.
