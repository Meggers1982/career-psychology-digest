## Emerging threads
- Hiring technology underperforms its sales pitch when tested: credential filters cut high-quality applicants (36.9%) and raise wages (W7203733318, 2026-08); AI rejections redirect blame onto the employer unless a human signs off (W7204209860, 2026-08); age-linked language gaps in AI video interviews shift with prompt wording (W7214200214, 2026-09); VR assessment appeal is age-skewed (W7202387608, 2026-08).
- The ChatGPT cheating panic in unproctored pre-employment testing is not visible in archival applicant score data, including among young applicants (W7202071941, 2026-08).
- Candidates read employer offers as character signals rather than economics: startup equity (W7204553807), humor in job ads lowering expected procedural justice (W7203608858), tuition-upfront vs. reimbursement design (W7211872890), CSR type vs. salary trade-offs (W7204088664).
- Diversity/fairness/performance trade-off appears structural in recruiting math, not just a management failure (W7213166433, 2026-09).
- Informal, relational conditions (flexible managers, approachable colleagues) may matter more than formal accommodation policy for ADHD and minority employees (W7208704204; W7213532045).
- Fairness perceptions of flexibility policies run across non-parents and prospective parents, not just parents ("anticipatory fairness," W7213900915).
- Individual-coping/resilience framings can entrench the barriers they address, in both gendered field occupations (W7204477379) and digital wellbeing tools (W4412125794).
- Involuntary career exit does not uniformly reduce work centrality — it can raise it (W7207518842).
- Onboarding help is a reciprocity exchange: helpers bear cost and withdraw acceptance when newcomers underperform (W7202177851).
- Screening of public social profiles imports legally off-limits attributes (religion) into interview decisions, amplified by current events (W7202344708).
