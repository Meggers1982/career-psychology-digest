# Topic Memory: Hiring & Selection
_Last updated: 2026-10-01_

## Established findings
- Formal policy consistently underperforms relational and lived reality in accommodation, neurodiversity and inclusion: visibility of a disability drives disbelief vs. over-assumption (W7211881958, 2026-09); informal relationships and flexible managers matter more than formal accommodations (W7208704204, 2026-09); inclusion policy vs. daily scrutiny gap for migrant women of color (W7207613776, 2026-09); "nowhereness" where neither work nor home is safe (W7213532045, 2026-09).
- Standardized selection testing is more robust than the current panic assumes: SJT validity holds across multimedia and constructed-response formats with lower cognitive load, N=244,257 meta-analysis (W7214648548, 2026-09); no ChatGPT-driven score inflation in archival applicant data (W7202071941, 2026-08).
- Candidates read employer offers and signals as character references rather than economics: startup equity (W7204553807), humor in job ads lowering expected procedural justice (W7203608858), tuition-upfront vs. reimbursement (W7211872890), CSR type vs. salary (W7204088664).

## Emerging threads
- Visible compliance is uninformative: newcomer "self-suppressive facades" under abusive supervision (W7203635941, 2026-08) and "moral camouflage" in green HR programs (W7172526535, 2026-08) both show participation masking disengagement and covert resistance.
- CSR/sustainability may be an attraction asset but a retention liability — attracts a values-segment externally (W7204088664) while moralized internal targets generate guilt (W7172526535). Watch for quantitative tests.
- Hiring technology underperforms its sales pitch when tested: filters cut high-quality applicants and raise wages (W7203733318); AI rejections redirect blame unless a human signs off (W7204209860); AI-interview age-language gaps shift with prompt wording (W7214200214); VR assessment appeal is age-skewed (W7202387608).
- Early-tenure experience as a hidden hiring cost: mistreatment in the first weeks converts engaged recruits into instrumental stayers (W7203635941); onboarding help is a reciprocity exchange that costs the helper (W7202177851).
- Diversity/fairness/performance trade-off looks structural in recruiting math, not merely a management failure (W7213166433).
- Individual-coping and resilience framings can entrench the barriers they address (W7204477379; W4412125794).
- Fairness judgments of flexibility policies extend to non-parents and prospective parents ("anticipatory fairness," W7213900915).
