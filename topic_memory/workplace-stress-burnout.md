## Emerging threads
- Buffering assumption under attack: demands and resources appear additive, not interactive, at scale (W7166081872, 2026-09); burnout reduction ran through cutting demands, not adding support (W7205707686, 2026-09); psychological capital/supervisor support protected life satisfaction but not burnout from bullying (W7211962843, 2026-09).
- Disparity-as-exposure framing: minority workers harmed by being concentrated in bad environments rather than by greater vulnerability to them; no interaction effects (W7213554539, 2026-09).
- Gamification is conditional, not inherently good or bad: competition features linked to work addiction/burnout/turnover (W7171963754, 2026-07), while gamification reduced burnout under low monitoring and perceived algorithmic fairness (W7172490991, 2026-08). Watch for replication and for spread from gig platforms to conventional employers.
- Structural levers over individual coping: schedule autonomy buffers family-to-work interruptions for remote workers, especially women (W7204753044, 2026-08); managers' own lack of decision authority and support flows downstream into employee virtual presenteeism (W7204491528, 2026-08).
- Daily recovery reconceived as a motivational problem: unfinished tasks lower leisure-related self-efficacy, not just detachment (W7213645268, 2026-09).
- Leader-team mismatch as a demand generator: leader proactivity exceeding the team's predicts overload and lower performance (W7172021448, 2026-07).
- Perception over objective difference in relational stressors: salience of leader-follower age gap, not the gap itself, predicts LMX, engagement, exhaustion (W7172442198, 2026-08).
- Low-cost self-directed interventions (five-day self-nudging/crafting protocols) show promise but with self-selection and follow-through problems (W7213538292, 2026-09).
