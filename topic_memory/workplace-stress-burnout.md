# Topic Memory: Workplace Stress & Burnout
_Last updated: 2026-10-14_

## Established findings
- Individual coping resources do not offset structural demands: demands and resources are additive at scale (W7166081872, 2026-09); burnout fell via demand reduction, not added support (W7205707686, 2026-09); psychological capital protected life satisfaction but not burnout (W7211962843, 2026-09); resilience only partially mediated harm from passive leadership (W7203948385, 2026-08); self-efficacy helped only on the resource side, sleep carried the demand side (W7213658467, 2026-09).
- Managerial strain is a downstream job demand for teams: squeezed managers → virtual presenteeism (W7204491528, 2026-08); leader-team proactivity mismatch → overload (W7172021448, 2026-07); overloaded managers go transactional and teams silently re-craft around them (W7215114401, 2026-10).
- Employee-initiated job crafting is the most consistently positive lever: participatory redesign (W7205707686), self-nudging protocols (W7213538292), AI-driven crafting (W7220357899, 2026-10).

## Emerging threads
- Generative AI and well-being: benefits tied to worker latitude over what gets automated/enriched; weakest effect on relatedness (W7220357899, 2026-10). Watch for causal/intervention designs and for mandated-rollout comparisons.
- Gamification/play is conditional and source-dependent: employer-imposed competition harmful (W7171963754, 2026-07); platform gamification helps only under perceived algorithmic fairness (W7172490991, 2026-08); employee-initiated playful work design lowers burnout via prevention focus, moderated by inclusive leadership (W7220405540, 2026-10). Prevention-focus mechanism needs replication.
- Withdrawal as coping, not character: quiet quitting predicted by weak group culture and sense of emptiness as independent drivers (W7220865997, 2026-10).
- Specificity and level of leadership effects: mental health-specific leadership beats generic support, but effects appear in individual perceptions with null team-level results (W7221041588, 2026-10); employee stigma moderates in opposite directions.
- Trait proactivity is contingent, not an asset per se: needs daily positive affect to convert into energy management (W7214949930, 2026-09); harmful when mismatched to team (W7172021448).
- Disparity-as-exposure framing for minority workers (W7213554539, 2026-09) — no new evidence this run; revisit or drop if unsupported next run.
- Daily recovery as a motivational problem (leisure-related self-efficacy, W7213645268, 2026-09), now with a physiological parallel in sleep quality (W7213658467).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-09-28: "The buffer myth collapses" — resilience/support cannot offset heavy demands; job redesign as the real lever (W7166081872, W7205707686, W7211962843, W7213554539, W7204753044, W7204491528). Targets: HBR, MIT SMR, The Atlantic, Fast Company.
- 2026-10-14: "The unofficial job redesign" — employees self-managing via AI crafting, private gamification, managing around stressed bosses, and withdrawal as the far end of the same spectrum (W7220357899, W7220405540, W7215114401, W7214949930, W7220865997). Targets: Wired, Business Insider, Fortune, Refinery29.
