## Emerging threads
- Leadership effects are being located in specific distributive acts (credit, blame, attention, candor) rather than in traits or styles: W7204196725 (delegation/blame-absorbing, 2026-08-25), W7221561375 (servant leadership + tough love, 2026-10-12), W7220937301 (differentiated transformational leadership, 2026-10-07).
- Servant leadership is recurring as a moderator/buffer against system-level harms rather than as a standalone style: W7214609077 (HPWS and deviance in hospitals), W7220913877 (restaurant service climate via group trust).
- Leadership as a fluctuating daily state, measured via experience sampling and within-leader experiments with direct-report ratings: W7212753349.
- Power amplifies leader behavior costs: coercive power turns leader anger into organizational dehumanization and withdrawal: W7220922862.
- Employee pushback against wellness-perk solutions in favor of procedural fairness and supportive leadership: W7206184378 (Indian IT, qualitative).
- Geographic spread beyond Western samples (Taiwan, Thailand, India, hospital multilevel data) — watch whether findings replicate in Western samples.
- Methodological caution: several findings rest on cross-sectional or two-wave self-report designs; scenario-experiment effects (credit sharing) have already failed to replicate in field data (W7204196725).
