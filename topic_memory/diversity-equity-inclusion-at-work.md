## Emerging threads
- Symbolic DEI levers underperform: gender-fair pronouns don't shift leader stereotypes (W7171908932, 2026-09), "Disability Confident" badges rarely appear as real commitments in job ads (W7212427136, 2026-09), diversity programs read as hollow or threatening to employees (W7214634718, 2026-09).
- Resources, not titles, as the real equity variable: men's authority comes with budget/staff, which drives meritocracy beliefs and reduces support for equity initiatives (W7203448124, 2026-08).
- Structural/infrastructural change beats persuasion — meta-analytic evidence from workplace pro-environmental interventions as an analog (W7213915259, 2026-09).
- Inclusion supports can create immobility: non-portable accommodations dampen ambition and external moves (W7214278713, 2026-09).
- Coping-focused interventions for discriminated-against employees (stress mindset, W7196926461, 2026-08) vs. stigma-unlearning sequencing (W7211977299, 2026-09) — watch for burden-shifting critique.
- AI in selection: recruiters report distrusting algorithms but are moved more by them than by human experts (W7218392398, 2026-10).
- Context dependence of DEI resistance and exclusion: national-history-patterned resistance among white male managers (W7197013324), linguistic ostracism in multilingual teams (W7204687022), motherhood discrimination and embeddedness in Japan (W7212085651).
- Anti-work orientation framed as a consequence of mistreatment rather than generational values (W7203777671, 2026-08).
