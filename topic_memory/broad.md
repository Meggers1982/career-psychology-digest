## Emerging threads
- Symbolic organizational signaling is decoupled from substance and often backfires: CEO activism without follow-through (W7208750164, 2026-09), self-focused moral talk by supervisors producing disgust (W7167868566, 2026-09), purpose statements that need engineered discomfort (W7172567426, 2026-09), unconventional job titles imposing legibility/verification costs (W7213226017, 2026-09).
- Daily recovery research is shifting from "how much rest" to sequence, timing and combination: evening activity profiles (W7169828568), piecewise daily energy curves with effort-dependent crashes (W7202007719), morning reflection framing as a randomized lever (W7207539887).
- Resource logic is being rethought from conservation toward generation — "mindful resourcing" spirals (W7204869548) — a counterweight to depletion-based burnout advice.
- Career self-management ideology under strain: proactive career behavior linked to employability and enthusiasm but also anxiety (W7204500576); decisional stress predicts avoidance, not planning, with progress (not pressure) dissolving stress (W7204791998).
- Structural, employer-controlled causes of disengagement: hierarchical career plateau → quiet quitting via lost felt obligation and meaning (W7204821646); repatriates penalized when fulfilled knowledge-transfer expectations lack recognition (W7212391861).
- Gender findings running against the backlash default: visionary female leaders rated more competent than men, strongest among male followers (W7212844454); women-only leadership program raised ambition via self-efficacy and reduced perceived gender barriers (W7208741244); caregiving-gap skills articulated as assets (W7212253583).
- Trust and access mechanics: structural-hole brokers face a credibility deficit that explicit network self-promotion only partly repairs (W7210270541).
- Family and household spillover into work: a relative's job search raises family-to-work conflict for the non-searcher (W7211937530); birth order shapes entry/exit in family firms (W7214180006).
- Selection on traits rather than results: risk preference tracks leadership status in humans and rank in chimpanzees, weaker in humans (W4414713988).
- Development-program design under question: coachee cognitive style predicts coaching-approach fit better than goal type (W7212149146); different support sources feed different career-adaptability dimensions in adolescents (W7204791763).
