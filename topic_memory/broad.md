# Topic Memory: Broad (all career and work psychology topics)
_Last updated: 2026-11-11_

## Established findings
- Exposure does not equal development: benefit requires active doing, scaffolding or structured reflection — playful-work-design exercises only worked if completed (W7214211158, 2026-09); hope/proactivity only paid off via occupational exploration (W7213930347, 2026-09); leader identity needed event binding (W7213545411, 2026-09); startup experience needed institutional scaffolding (W7208810968, 2026-10); hands-on CTE students named concrete callings (W7212238392, 2026-09).
- Evaluation by prototype or aggregate produces exclusion with no identifiable villain: unbiased personality tests depress autistic scores on screened traits (W7207655302); gender-diverse workers lose ground via attitudes not policy (W7211865783); founder appearance drives "legitimacy" judgments (W7214669005, 2026-09); broad-band ethnicity reporting hides subgroup absence at FTSE top (W7220681324, 2026-10); impact capital tracks proximity over need (W7211933722).
- Leadership effects are contingent on the receiver, not the sender: vision works only via perceived plausibility and harmonious passion (W7204870087); moral framing depletes the most morally attentive (W7220998881, 2026-10); servant leadership fuels workaholism in perfectionistic self-presenters (W7219177765, 2026-10); feedback impact hinges on novelty/disruptiveness (W7207697928).
- Insecurity and economic pressure change behavior, not just mood: insecurity suppresses help-seeking and raises cyberloafing (W7204954329); financial insecurity raises anger and counterproductive behavior more than it lowers task performance (W7215113460, 2026-10); downturn framing, not perceived severity, governs whether managers still voice opportunities (W7202103093, 2026-11).

## Emerging threads
- Informal standing outranks formal authority: status precedes power via communality (W7214338919, 2026-09); advice centrality drives leadership emergence (W7214315853, 2026-09); political skill works only through LMX (W7212394179, 2026-09); domain experts hold the AI deployment veto (W7221037317, 2026-10).
- AI adoption as an epistemic/political problem rather than a technical or explainability one (W7221037317) — watch for replication outside banking and outside India.
- Disaggregation is replacing single-score constructs: five motives for career delay (W7214312495, 2026-09); ethnic subgroup splits (W7220681324); earlier bullying and support profiles (W7207619744, W7212183752).
- Underuse is not uniformly corrosive: boredom co-occurs with mood and conduct decline (W7204556045) but overqualification routes into generativity and helping for older workers given an outlet (W7213969383, 2026-09).
- Entrepreneurship research shifting to founder psychology and hybrid work: dark-triad founders discount likability and react to equity dilution (W7220450586, 2026-10); hybrid entrepreneurship looks like a stable state, not a runway (W7214931138, 2026-09).
- Embodiment and life stage entering career research — perimenopause "identity pause" and somatic attunement (W7220928682, 2026-10); plus gendered burdens surfacing within supposedly neutral mechanisms (W7214315853, W7220681324).
- Gig/platform labor and collective representation as a buffer against customer harassment spillover (W7218644956, 2026-10) — first appearance, watch for more.
