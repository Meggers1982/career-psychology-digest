## Emerging threads
- Selection and allocation follow evaluation cost, not merit or need: unbiased personality tests still depress autistic candidates on screened traits (W7207655302, 2026-09); gender-diverse workers lose ground at every lifecycle stage via attitudes not policy (W7211865783, 2026-09); firms open nontraditional pipelines by imitating rivals (W7204877748, 2026-09); impact capital tracks proximity over need (W7211933722, 2026-09).
- Experience does not self-execute into development: leadership experience only builds leader identity with high event binding capacity (W7213545411, 2026-09); startup experience only boosts employability when scaffolded (W7208810968, 2026-10); hands-on CTE students articulate callings more concretely (W7212238392, 2026-09); first-gen support profiles show family vs. campus support are non-substitutable (W7212183752, 2026-09).
- Leadership communication is judged by receivers, not senders: rejecting a good idea costs competence-based trust with observers (W7159739744, 2026-09); vision works only through perceived plausibility and only for harmoniously passionate followers (W7204870087, 2026-09); feedback impact hinges on novelty/disruptiveness, not content (W7207697928, 2026-09).
- Underload and insecurity as neglected risks: rising boredom co-occurs with depressed mood, procrastination and counterproductive behavior, buffered by job control (W7204556045, 2026-08); job insecurity suppresses help-seeking and raises cyberloafing (W7204954329, 2026-09).
- Person-centered methods are displacing average scores (bullying profiles W7207619744; first-gen support profiles W7212183752; fsQCA employability configurations W7208810968) — watch whether findings replicate outside single organizations.
- Body-clock and timing interventions as cheap well-being levers: morning blue light improving next-day alertness via sleep (W7207621701, 2026-09) — links to earlier daily-rhythm work (W7169828568, W7202007719, W7207539887).
- Meaning at work is narrowing to the job for younger cohorts (W7213869505, 2026-09), raising employer-held identity risk.
- Structural backing enables agency: assigned expatriates respond to career shocks more proactively than self-initiated movers (W7204712971, 2026-08) — counterweight to boundaryless-career ideology (prior: W7204500576, W7204791998, W7204821646).
- Symbolic organizational signaling decoupled from substance and backfiring (prior run: W7208750164, W7167868566, W7172567426, W7213226017); ambiguity itself can be monetized (W7213242733, 2026-09).
- Gender findings running against the backlash default (prior run: W7212844454, W7208741244, W7212253583) — watch for replication.
