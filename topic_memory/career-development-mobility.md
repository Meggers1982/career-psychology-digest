# Topic Memory: Career Development & Mobility
_Last updated: 2026-11-14_

## Established findings
- Job crafting's payoff is conditional, not automatic: it drains people in unstable/ambiguous roles (W7221798889, 2026-10), requires personal resources (W7223306094, 2026-10), is recognized by supervisors only in task form (W7221245981, 2026-10), and at team level needs empowering leadership plus psychological safety (W7223171732, 2026-10).
- Insight and mindset translate into career outcomes only through enacted behavior: career competencies via career crafting (W7222470451, 2026-10); protean/boundaryless orientations via planning and learning (W7223829068, 2026-10).
- Leadership and climate are repeatedly the enabling condition for individual career agency: inclusive leadership and calling reciprocally linked (W7221320159, 2026-10); psychological safety as moderator (W7223171732).
- Curvilinear patterns in mobility decisions: U-shaped CEO retirement (W7223031859, 2026-10); inverted-U job complexity and turnover intent via identity threat (W7222847257, 2026-10).

## Emerging threads
- Structural/contextual gating of career advice: exploration converts to commitment for men only, with family and opportunity constraints implicated (W7226022739, 2026-11); openness to move is inert in constrained labor markets (W7223829068). Watch for studies that measure context directly instead of proxying it by gender.
- Career adaptability is being unpacked rather than used as a predictor: weak cross-lagged links among its four dimensions (W7225739101, 2026-11), reciprocal with problematic smartphone use via academic cynicism (W7223817463, 2026-10).
- Pre-employment and adolescent samples entering the career-mobility literature; digital distraction as a career-motivation phenomenon.
- Deliberate non-linearity (planned gap years) as a marker of agency rather than drift (W7225739101).
- "Career inaction" as a distinct object (W7223284391, 2026-10); belief systems about how promotions happen as upstream of engagement, segmented by gender and age (W7221331977, 2026-10).
- Persistent method caution: single-country, self-report, cross-sectional or cross-lagged designs dominate; within-person (RI-CLPM) effects weaker than headline results (W7221320159).

## Feature ideas already pitched
- 2026-10-30: "Companies handed career development back to employees" — self-managed careers/job crafting work only for well-resourced people in legible roles (W7221798889, W7223306094, W7221245981, W7222470451, W7223284391). Pitched to HBR, Fast Company, The Atlantic, Refinery29.
- 2026-11-14: "A future you can picture" — adolescent future-orientation as the lever behind phone addiction, gap-year agency, and unequal payoff from exploration (W7223817463, W7225739101, W7226022739). Pitched to Vox, Business Insider, Time, Parents.
