## Emerging threads
- Resistance to AI at work centers on authority and moral standing, not competence: AI judged unfit as leader but fine as subordinate (W7168435596, 2026-09-21); outsiders penalize firms that give AI final say in HR, inferring objectification of employees (W7204855159, 2026-08-31).
- Algorithmic management reshapes identity and relational behavior: "algorithmic identity regulation" among Indian delivery partners (W7213334889, 2026-09-15); algorithmic control linked to dehumanizing customers, buffered by customer gratitude (W7218741843, 2026-10-03).
- Workers' *interpretation* of algorithms matters as much as algorithm design: autonomy and fairness legitimacy raise proactive service, privacy legitimacy negatively related; peer support unexpectedly dampened the effect (W7204647820, 2026-08-29).
- Contradictory evidence on whether AI awareness mobilizes or paralyzes careers: AI awareness → impostor thoughts → proactive career behavior when future work self is vivid (W7223279808, 2026-10-23) vs. STARA awareness weakening the future-work-self → career crafting → employability chain (W7213541995, 2026-09-18). Worth watching for replication/resolution.
- Identity work, not tool training, is the hard part of AI adoption in creative/knowledge occupations: resisting, extending, crafting (W7220851400, 2026-10-23).
- Digital/remote-capable work as a new inequality axis: immigrants concentrated in digitally intensive but low-telework-feasibility industries, incomplete earnings convergence over 50 years (W7222280836, 2026-10-15).
- Methodological caution: this field is dominated by three-wave self-report surveys in single countries (UK, China, India, Italy); causal and cross-national evidence is thin.
