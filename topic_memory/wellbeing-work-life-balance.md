## Emerging threads
- Work strain is repeatedly documented as spilling into the home domain rather than into visible job performance: leader perfectionism (W7167993239), customer cyber incivility (W7204489065), daily workgroup exclusion (W7220972684), all 2026-08/10.
- Work-life "choice" is being dismantled: integration framed as survival inside high-responsiveness cultures (W7211998375) and "de-agentic boundary management" when crises remove tactic choice (W7213366869), 2026-09.
- Individual-level remedies questioned in favor of structural ones: digital competence only protects when processes and top-team connectivity norms exist (W7214892649); perceived relevance can substitute for participatory design in health interventions (W7204244254).
- Gender and household context as under-recognized moderators of wellbeing policy: women's work-family conflict rises when they out-earn partners (W7203464894); gender-matched mentoring improves relationship warmth but not work outcomes (W7204030144).
- Counterintuitive buffering effects awaiting replication: leader identification weakens, rather than strengthens, transmission of workaholism (W7204799973); non-additive CQ facet interactions (W7211941550).
- Team-level distribution of effort, not just volume, linked to shared potency beliefs (W7203699710) — watch for replication outside possible student samples.
