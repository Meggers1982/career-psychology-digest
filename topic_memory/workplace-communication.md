## Emerging threads
- Two complementary explanations for "whose voice carries": leader-created insider/outsider climate (W7212150272, 2026-09-10, JMP) and numerical representation thresholds (W7212172629, 2026-09-10, JAP). Watch for a study that tests both together.
- Voice splits by type — promotive (ideas) rises and prohibitive (risk reporting) falls under narcissistic leadership (W7212150272). Awaiting replication outside China and non-employee-rated leader measures.
- Critical mass tipping point ~29% representation (SD 6.67), with moderators: minority members in leadership lower the threshold, internal division within the minority group weakens effects (W7212172629). Watch for a true pooled meta-analysis to replace vote-counting.
- Possible spillover channel: leader work-family balance predicting less favoritism in team climate (W7212150272). Track whether manager overload/recovery links to communication fairness elsewhere.
- Standing measurement caution: this literature remains time-lagged correlational with common-source employee reports; reviews exclude weak designs in ways that may inflate support.
