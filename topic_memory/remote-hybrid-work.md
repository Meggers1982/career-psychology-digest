## Emerging threads
- Peer-level ties, not supervisor relationships, appear to be the durable support structure in remote/hybrid and post-RTO settings: bottom-up psychological safety (W7203941475), teammate compassion outlasting supervisor dependability six months after return (W7204438985), low-obligation "work companions" in coworking spaces (W7202178589).
- Cooperative temperament may outperform assertiveness in distributed teams: agreeableness as a necessary condition across all high-performing configurations in global virtual teams (W7169580639) — student sample, needs replication in employee teams.
- Return-to-office payoff is slow, not immediate: job reattachment linked to performance only at six months, not right after return (W7204438985).
- Flexibility perks are contingent on job design, not universally beneficial: compressed workweeks helped high-autonomy, low-interdependence workers and eroded perceived support for low-autonomy workers (W7213775189).
- Methodological note: this batch leans heavily qualitative/single-site (two Organization Science studies) or single-country (Indian IT); watch for larger quantitative or multi-country replications.
