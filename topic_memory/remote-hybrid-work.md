## Emerging threads
- Peer-level ties as durable support structure in remote/hybrid and post-RTO work: bottom-up psychological safety (W7203941475), teammate compassion outlasting supervisor dependability at six months (W7204438985), low-obligation "work companions" in coworking spaces (W7202178589).
- DIRECT COUNTERWEIGHT (new, 2026-11-01): formal directive leadership — structure, roles, support systems — predicted *more* shared leadership via team empowerment in 70 virtual teams at two Fortune 500 firms, strongest in the most dispersed, text-based teams (W7224124147). Open question for future runs: are direction and peer leadership complements rather than substitutes?
- Communication richness as a moderator: lean, text-mediated teams may need more formal structure than video-rich ones (W7224124147). Watch for replication.
- Cooperative temperament may outperform assertiveness in distributed teams (W7169580639, student sample) — now in tension with the directive-leadership finding; needs employee-sample replication.
- RTO payoff is slow, not immediate: reattachment–performance link appeared only at six months (W7204438985).
- Flexibility perks are contingent on job design: compressed workweeks helped high-autonomy, low-interdependence workers, eroded perceived support for low-autonomy workers (W7213775189).
- Methodological note: topic output volume is low (5 screened, 1 reported this run). Evidence base still skews self-report, single-country or single-site; larger multi-firm quantitative work (W7224124147) is the exception, not the rule.
