### Study 1: Narcissistic bosses hear the good ideas and silence the bad news
**Work ID:** W7212150272 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Moderator reported as main effect** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "Leaders who reported better work-family balance were less likely to foster that insider-outsider climate."
- **Abstract says:** "leaders' work-family balance weakens the positive association between narcissistic leadership and team chaxu climate"
- **Problem:** The abstract describes work-family balance as a boundary condition that buffers the narcissism-to-chaxu-climate relationship, not as a general predictor of less favoritism. As written, readers could conclude that any leader with better balance builds a fairer team, which the abstract does not support.
- **Suggested fix:** "Rewrite as: "Among narcissistic leaders, those with better work-family balance were less likely to translate that narcissism into an insider-outsider climate \u2014 the study frames balance as a buffering condition, not a general driver of fairness.""

**Issue B. Same moderator error repeated in story angle** — Severity: Moderate

- **As written:** "The finding that leaders with better work-family balance built less favoritism into their teams also hints that an overloaded manager's home life spills into how fairly they treat people."
- **Abstract says:** "leaders' work-family balance weakens the positive association between narcissistic leadership and team chaxu climate"
- **Problem:** Repeats the main-effect framing of what the abstract reports as a moderation, and adds a causal spillover reading ("home life spills into") for a time-lagged correlational survey.
- **Suggested fix:** "Reframe as a conditional, non-causal observation, e.g. "Work-family balance appeared to blunt the link between narcissistic leadership and favoritism, hinting at \u2014 but not demonstrating \u2014 a connection between a manager's home life and how they treat their team.""

**Issue C. Embellished definition of chaxu climate** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "a "chaxu" team climate \u2014 one in which the leader openly divides the team into favored insiders and outsiders"
- **Abstract says:** "team chaxu climate mediates the relationships between narcissistic leadership and both forms of employee voice"
- **Problem:** The abstract gives no definition of chaxu climate; the word "openly" adds a claim about visibility of the favoritism that cannot be checked from the abstract.
- **Suggested fix:** "Drop "openly" or mark the definition as a general gloss on the construct rather than a study finding."

**Issue D. Source of work-family balance measure** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Leaders who reported better work-family balance"
- **Abstract says:** "Data were collected from 408 employees and 68 supervisors in China using a three-wave time-lagged survey."
- **Problem:** The abstract does not specify whether work-family balance was leader-reported or rated by employees. Unverifiable from abstract only.
- **Suggested fix:** "Use neutral wording such as "leaders with better work-family balance" unless the source of the rating can be confirmed from the full text."
