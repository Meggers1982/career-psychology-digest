### Study 2: When the algorithm is your boss and your reputation is your paycheck
**Work ID:** W7224565098 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Unnamed sources of social support** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Informal social support — peer networks, family, fellow freelancers — helped people cope and develop capabilities"
- **Abstract says:** "informal social support functions as an extra-platform capability infrastructure ... informal social support contributes directly to capability development"
- **Problem:** The abstract does not specify who provides the informal support; the list of peers, family and fellow freelancers is added detail that cannot be checked here.
- **Suggested fix:** "Write simply 'Informal social support helped people cope and develop capabilities' or mark the examples as unverifiable from the abstract."

**Issue B. 'Generative AI' specification** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "for a growing class of knowledge freelancers, generative AI is not an abstract future threat but an active force devaluing their existing skills"
- **Abstract says:** "artificial intelligence (AI)-driven market changes constrain capabilities through skill devaluation"
- **Problem:** The abstract refers to AI-driven market changes generally, not specifically to generative AI.
- **Suggested fix:** "Change 'generative AI' to 'AI-driven market change'."

**Issue C. Claim that disputing a job lowers ratings** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "a rating system that penalizes anyone who disputes a job"
- **Abstract says:** "digital enforcement and dispute processes undermine procedural fairness and recognition"
- **Problem:** The abstract says dispute processes undermine fairness and recognition, but does not state that raising a dispute lowers a freelancer's rating. Unverifiable from abstract only.
- **Suggested fix:** "Rephrase to 'a rating system built on dispute processes that freelancers describe as procedurally unfair' or similar."

**Issue D. Unappealable reputation damage** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "a reputation score that can be damaged in ways you cannot appeal"
- **Abstract says:** "digital enforcement and dispute processes undermine procedural fairness and recognition"
- **Problem:** The absence of any appeal route is an inference; the abstract only reports that procedural fairness is undermined. Unverifiable from abstract only.
- **Suggested fix:** "Soften to 'a reputation score governed by processes freelancers experience as procedurally unfair'."
