### Study 1: Workers who use AI to redesign their own jobs report more meaningful days
**Work ID:** W7220357899 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Differential effects flattened** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "On days workers used AI in these ways, they reported more satisfaction of psychological needs — especially competence — which linked to greater work meaningfulness and engagement."
- **Abstract says:** "the dimensions of Gen-AI-driven job crafting (augmenting instrumental resources, augmenting social resources, augmenting challenging demands, and automating hindering demands) were differentially associated with daily work engagement through need satisfaction"
- **Problem:** The abstract stresses that the four crafting dimensions worked differently; the digest implies all four produced the same benefit, with only the competence/relatedness contrast preserved.
- **Suggested fix:** "Add that the four forms were 'differentially associated' with engagement rather than uniformly beneficial."

**Issue B. Untested extrapolation to mandated rollouts** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Mandated, top-down AI rollouts may miss the engagement benefits that come from employees crafting their own use."
- **Abstract says:** "these findings suggest that Gen-AI may function as a bottom-up work design tool associated with daily need satisfaction and engagement"
- **Problem:** The study only observed self-initiated use among voluntary heavy users; it made no comparison with mandated or top-down AI use, so the contrast is speculation.
- **Suggested fix:** "Flag explicitly that no mandated-use condition was studied, e.g. 'the study did not compare self-directed with mandated use'."

### Study 5: Making work playful cuts burnout — but through caution, not fun
**Work ID:** W7220405540 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Causal headline for correlational design** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Making work playful cuts burnout — but through caution, not fun"
- **Abstract says:** "prevention focus mediated the negative relationship between PWD and job burnout"
- **Problem:** 'Cuts burnout' states a causal effect; the design is a three-wave self-report survey showing associations, as the digest's own caveats acknowledge.
- **Suggested fix:** "Rephrase to 'Playful work design is linked to lower burnout — via caution, not fun'."

**Issue B. Direction of moderation not in abstract** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Inclusive leadership strengthened both indirect pathways."
- **Abstract says:** "Inclusive leadership moderated the positive indirect relationship between PWD and thriving at work via promotion focus. It also moderated the negative indirect relationship between PWD and job burnout via prevention focus."
- **Problem:** The abstract reports only that inclusive leadership moderated the two indirect paths; it does not state that it strengthened them. The same unsupported direction is repeated in the manager angle ('benefits appeared strongest where leaders were inclusive').
- **Suggested fix:** "Say inclusive leadership 'moderated' both indirect pathways, or note that the direction of the moderation is not specified in the abstract."

### Study 7: Absent leadership drains police officers — and resilience only partly cushions the fall
**Work ID:** W7203948385 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Direction of effects inferred** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "officers under passive leaders reported lower resilience, which in turn tracked with less dedication and more exhaustion"
- **Abstract says:** "The results suggest that resilience partially mediates the relationship between laissez-faire leadership and work dedication and laissez-faire leadership and emotional exhaustion."
- **Problem:** The abstract reports partial mediation without specifying the sign of any path; the digest supplies directions that are plausible but unverifiable from the abstract alone.
- **Suggested fix:** "State that resilience partially explained the links between passive leadership and both dedication and exhaustion, without asserting the specific signs, or note the directions are implied rather than reported."
