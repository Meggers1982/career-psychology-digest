### Study 1: Pay gaps inside the executive suite boost digital transformation — until they don't
**Work ID:** W7222968425 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Causal verb in headline** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Pay gaps inside the executive suite boost digital transformation — until they don't"
- **Abstract says:** "Equity-based incentive exposure and internal pay dispersion are positively associated with DT ... Internal pay dispersion exhibits an inverted U-shaped relationship with DT"
- **Problem:** "Boost" asserts causation, while the study reports associations from fixed-effects panel models; the body text and caveats correctly hedge, so the headline is out of step with the rest of the entry.
- **Suggested fix:** "Reword to something associational, e.g. "Pay gaps inside the executive suite track with more digital transformation — until they don't"."

**Issue B. Coordination mechanism treated as observed** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "past a point, the spread appears to correspond with worse coordination on big company-wide projects, not better effort"
- **Abstract says:** "suggesting that moderate tournament incentives may mobilize managerial effort, while excessive dispersion may weaken coordination"
- **Problem:** The abstract offers weakened coordination as a tentative interpretation of the inverted-U shape; coordination itself was not measured. The digest presents it as something the data correspond with.
- **Suggested fix:** "Add hedging, e.g. "the authors suggest the spread may start to undermine coordination, though coordination itself wasn't measured"."
