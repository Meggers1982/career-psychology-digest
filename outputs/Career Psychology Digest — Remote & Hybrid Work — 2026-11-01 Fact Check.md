### Study 1: In scattered virtual teams, being a directive boss is what unlocks shared leadership
**Work ID:** W7224124147 | **Verdict:** ⚠️ Minor issues

**Issue A. Causal headline for a field study** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "In scattered virtual teams, being a directive boss is what unlocks shared leadership"
- **Abstract says:** "A 10-week field study of 392 team members from 70 teams across two Fortune 500 companies supports the proposed model: directive leadership enhances shared leadership indirectly through team empowerment."
- **Problem:** The headline states a definite causal mechanism ("is what unlocks") from a non-experimental field study; the digest's own body and caveats correctly hedge to "was associated with" and note causal direction is not established, so the headline overshoots the rest of the entry.
- **Suggested fix:** "Soften to something like "In scattered virtual teams, directive bosses are linked to more shared leadership.""

**Issue B. Country of sample not stated** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Two large US companies only, limiting generalizability"
- **Abstract says:** "two Fortune 500 companies"
- **Problem:** The abstract identifies the companies only as Fortune 500 and says nothing about where the teams or members were located; in virtual teams with high location diversity, participants may well be outside the US. Unverifiable from abstract only.
- **Suggested fix:** "Change to "Two Fortune 500 companies only, limiting generalizability.""

**Issue C. Expanded definition of directive leadership** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "Directive leadership — setting structure, clarifying roles and establishing support systems —"
- **Abstract says:** "formal leaders in virtual teams can foster shared leadership by using directive behaviors that establish structural support systems"
- **Problem:** "Clarifying roles" and "setting structure" are the digest's own gloss; the abstract only mentions directive behaviors that establish structural support systems. The same embellishment carries into the manager story angle ("clear roles, explicit norms and decision rules").
- **Suggested fix:** "Describe directive leadership as "behaviors that establish structural support systems" and note that the specific behaviors measured are not detailed in the abstract."

**Issue D. Perceived vs. objective media richness** — Severity: Minor

- **As written:** "those spread across many locations and relying on lean, text-based communication rather than rich media"
- **Abstract says:** "This indirect effect is amplified when location diversity is high and perceived media richness is low."
- **Problem:** The moderator is perceived media richness, a team-member perception, not an objective measure of channel use; "relying on chat and email" is an interpretation the abstract does not make.
- **Suggested fix:** "Say the effect was strongest when location diversity was high and members perceived their communication media as low in richness."
